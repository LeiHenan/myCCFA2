 Contributor 
 Overview:
 Add a disaggregated KV-aware routing launcher for SGLang sidecars and align the existing sidecar launchers with Dynamo's standard configuration.
 Details:
 Add disagg_kv_router.sh with two prefill and two decode SGLang engines, four native-gRPC sidecars, KV-event discovery, and Dynamo's KV-aware frontend router.
 Give every engine distinct GPU, HTTP, gRPC, KV-event, and system-port defaults, plus distinct bootstrap ports for the two prefill engines.
 Make the first aggregate sidecar honor DYN_SYSTEM_PORT1 , then fall back to DYN_SYSTEM_PORT , then 8081 .
 Remove unused OTEL_SERVICE_NAME assignments from the aggregate KV-router and disaggregated launchers.
 Validate the launchers with bash -n , ShellCheck, help/error-path checks, and the applicable pre-commit hooks.
 Where should the reviewer start?
