### CLOSED-7: Attention sinks are provably necessary (theory)
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11487
- Evidence: READ BODY
- Detail: Verbatim from the abstract: "We prove that, in some settings, it is the latter: computing a trigger-conditional behavior necessarily induces a sink in softmax self-attention models." and "We also prove that non-normalized ReLU attention can solve the same task without any sink, confirming that the normalization constraint is the fundamental driver of sink behavior." This closes the question of whether the sink is merely an optimization artifact — a result relevant to any eviction policy that treats position-0 as a disposable token.

### CLOSED-8: Recency/Frequency (ARC-style) adaptive KV block caching for vLLM
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21238
- Evidence: READ BODY
- Detail: Verbatim from the abstract: "Existing cache management strategies adopt the least-recently-used policy for evicting cache blocks. However, LRU leads to multiple unrelated workloads flushing each other's caches. To address this, we integrate adaptive caching that dynamically allocates cache space between recently and frequently occurring KV blocks." Reported effect, verbatim: "it improves the KV cache hit rate by up to 10.8% and reduces time to first token by up to 12.6% over naive vLLM on synthetic document question answering workloads, and 2.1% and 2.0% respectively on real-world conversation workloads." Publication venue, verbatim: "Accepted at the ICML 2026 Workshop on Resource-Adaptive Foundation Model Inference (AdaptFM)". Note the small real-workload gains (2.1% / 2.0%) versus the 10.8% / 12.6% synthetic figures.

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16824

- WHO is still asking: `sjmshsh` (author) in vLLM; issue state is `OPEN` with `"stateReason":"REOPENED"`, label `feature request`, created 2026-04-19, last updated 2026-08-21.
- URL: https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/40268
- Verbatim quote of the asking: "vLLM's GPU KV cache (`BlockPool`) uses a **single-queue LRU** implemented in`FreeKVCacheBlockQueue`. - What specifically is missing: no `gpu_eviction_policy` flag exists. Confirmed against the local vLLM tree: `vllm/v1/core/block_pool.py` still holds exactly one `self.free_block_queue = FreeKVCacheBlockQueue(self.blocks)` and no policy registry; the pluggable `CachePolicyFactory` exists only under `vllm/v1/kv_offload/cpu/policies/` (offload tier), not for HBM blocks.

### OPEN-7: Eviction across replicated KV tiers has no principled policy (published open problem)
- WHO is still asking: the authors of "From Tensor Buffer to Distributed Memory Hierarchy: A Survey of KV Cache Management for LLM Serving" (arXiv 2607.02574), §6 design-gap agenda.
- URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02574v1
- Verbatim quote of the asking: "DG3—Eviction across replicated tiers. A KV block can be live in HBM, host DRAM, and a remote store simultaneously. Existing tier-aware systems often approximate this with per-tier heuristics. MG4 and MG5 are therefore prerequisites."
- Verbatim quote of the measurement gap underneath it: "Reuse-distance and block-lifetime distributions are also absent (MG4), which leaves eviction policies tuned by intuition rather than workload evidence."
- What specifically is missing: per-block reuse-distance and lifetime traces (MG4) and prefetchability slack (MG5); the survey states these are preconditions for the policy, not optional niceties.
- Evidence: READ BODY

### OPEN-8: Per-head sparse KV eviction (H2O-style) is not representable in vLLM's block manager
- WHO is still asking: a vLLM user asking on the official vLLM forum ("Sparse attention (e.g., H2O)", topic 2745, category KV-Cache). The thread contains the user's own partial workaround, which itself is the answer that the feature is missing.
