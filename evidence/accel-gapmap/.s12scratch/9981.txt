--- COMMENT 2 ---
Hi @alrolo3,
I regret to inform you that GPT-OSS-120B currently does not support the TensorRT backend. It only supports the PyTorch backend.
Additionally, it requires the Blackwell architecture, which means that the A100-SXM4-80GB (Ampere architecture) is not compatible.

For reference,:
- [Supported Models](https://nvidia.github.io/TensorRT-LLM/models/supported-models.html#supported-models)
- [Deployment Guide for GPT-OSS on TensorRT-LLM - Blackwell Hardware](https://nvidia.github.io/TensorRT-LLM/deployment-guide/deployment-guide-for-gpt-oss-on-trtllm.html)

--- COMMENT 3 ---
Closing this as the comments above should have addressed it. Please open a new one if it persists on the latest version.  Additionally, please be noted that the TensorRT backend has been deprecated. :)
