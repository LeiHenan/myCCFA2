--- COMMENT 1 ---
Summary

 flashinfer 0.6.17+ auto-selects fc2 tile_n=512/tile_k=32 for small-m W4A16 decode, then rejects that tile in its own force_tile_config re-validation ( tile_k>=64 floor), so CUDA-graph warmup fails on SM120 for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B W4A16 (m=3/4).

 Wrap flashinfer's _candidate_tile_fits in CuteDslB12xFusedMoE (W4A16_NVFP4 only) to accept exactly that tile under the override's own rule; everything else passes through. Gated on flashinfer >= 0.6.17, no-op once upstream ships _ultra_wide_fc2_tile_fits . No flashinfer pin change.

 Unwaive TestQwen3_6_35B_A3B::test_nvfp4_w4a16 .

 Test Coverage

 tests/unittest/_torch/moe/test_cute_dsl_b12x_moe_backend.py : 3 new cpu_only tests.

 RTX PRO 6000: test_nvfp4_w4a16 reproduces the CI error without the shim and passes with it.
