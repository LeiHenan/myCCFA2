# Qwen3-30B-A3B NVFP4 Evaluation Results (MoE, reasoning)

## Setup

**Baseline** — `Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B` (BF16)

**Variants** — 12 NVFP4 W4A4 models, all `nvfp4-pack-quantized`, group_size 16, `tensor_group` strategy:

- All 48 `mlp.gate` routers and `lm_head` left unquantized.
- GPTQ variants additionally use `actorder: static` on weights.
- `-ultrachat` variants are calibrated on UltraChat; all others on the default perfect-blend mix.
- Plain `NVFP4` is round-to-nearest on the weights — its calibration pass only fits the activation global scales.

**Seeds** — 1234, 2345, 3456. aime25 additionally uses 4567, 5678, 6789, 7890, 8901.

**Sampling** — temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95, top_k 20

**Serving** — vLLM v0.28.0, TP=1 (one GPU per model), max-model-len 40960, no YaRN, `--reasoning-parser qwen3`

**Harness** — eval-agent `--category reasoning`, num_concurrent 32

**Recovery** — seed-mean(quantized) / seed-mean(BF16), per task, on the metric listed for that task. Every score in this document is a mean over seeds.

## Recommended models

Ranked by mean recovery over the five non-gsm8k tasks (mmlu_pro_chat, ifeval, math_500, gpqa_diamond, aime25).

| Rank | Model | Mean recovery (5 tasks) | Worst task | Tasks &ge; 0.99 |
|------|-------|-------------------------|------------|-----------------|
| 1 | `NVFP4-GPTQ-AWQ-ultrachat` | 0.9849 | 0.9536 (gpqa_diamond) | 2 / 5 |
| 2 | `NVFP4-AWQ` | 0.9811 | 0.9459 (gpqa_diamond) | 2 / 5 |
| 3 | `NVFP4-GPTQ-ultrachat` | 0.9800 | 0.9588 (aime25) | 2 / 5 |

- `NVFP4-GPTQ-AWQ-ultrachat` — **Default pick.** Best mean of any variant, and best mean rank across the five tasks (4.2 of 12). Holds the best aime25 in the sweep (0.9882) and 2nd-best mmlu_pro (0.9986); its softest column is math_500 (0.9883, rank 10 of 12), which is the one thing 2 covers better.
- `NVFP4-AWQ` — **Math-heavy workloads.** Best math_500 anywhere (1.0262) and best ifeval of the three (0.9969), but the weakest mmlu_pro of the three by a wide margin (0.9601 vs 0.9986) — do not ship it where knowledge/multiple-choice matters.
- `NVFP4-GPTQ-ultrachat` — **Highest floor.** Its worst task (0.9588) is the best worst-task of all 12 variants, and it holds the best gpqa in the sweep (0.9742). Pick it when the requirement is "nothing regresses badly" rather than a peak on any one task.

Caveats: 2 and 3 are separated by 0.0011 mean recovery, which is inside the noise on every task that drives it — treat them as tied on aggregate and choose on the profile above. Dropping gsm8k is what promotes `NVFP4-AWQ` into the top three; on gsm8k it is the third-weakest variant (0.9754), so keep it out of the running if gsm8k-style CoT arithmetic ever matters. `NVFP4-GPTQ-AWQ-ultrachat` leads with or without gsm8k.

## Takeaways

- **`NVFP4-GPTQ-AWQ-ultrachat` is the pick** — best mean recovery (0.9885), wins gsm8k (1.0067) and aime25 (0.9882).
- **The BF16 gap is concentrated in gpqa_diamond and aime25.** Mean recovery across the 12 variants: gpqa 0.9439 and aime25 0.9451 — 5.6 and 5.5 pts short of BF16 — vs mmlu_pro 0.9787, gsm8k 0.9813, ifeval 0.9934 and math_500 1.0010 (no loss). Those two are the only tasks where NVFP4 gives up real ground, and they are the worst task for 11 of 12 variants.
- **GPTQ is what closes that gap.** On gpqa the top four variants are all GPTQ recipes (0.9742 / 0.9639 / 0.9588 / 0.9536, vs 0.9510 for the best non-GPTQ), and a GPTQ recipe tops aime25 too (0.9882, though 2 there is plain AWQ at 0.9765). Mean rank of the 6 GPTQ vs 6 non-GPTQ variants is 4.8 vs 8.2 on gpqa and 5.7 vs 7.3 on aime25. The individual deltas sit inside those tasks' noise floors (±0.0490, ±0.0807), but the ranking is consistent across both — the one exception is `GPTQ+SmoothQuant`, last on both (0.9046, 0.9059).
- **GPTQ's other effects:** its one resolvable win elsewhere is gsm8k (+0.0280 averaged over the 6 recipe pairs, positive in 6 of 6, 2.5x the noise floor); it costs mmlu_pro (−0.0102, ~3x that task's floor) and math_500 (−0.0131).
- **UltraChat vs perfect-blend is a wash on average (+0.0038) but directional per task:** clearly better on mmlu_pro (+0.0231, positive for all 6 recipes), clearly worse on math_500 (−0.0148). Use UltraChat unless math is the target.
- **Calibration matters most for the smoothing recipes.** GPTQ+AWQ swings 0.9720 → 0.9885 on calibration alone — largest spread in the sweep. GPTQ is indifferent (0.0004). RTN is the recipe UltraChat hurts most (−0.0130), which fits: it has no per-channel scales to fit.
- **Recipe order:** GPTQ+AWQ (0.9902) > AWQ (0.9888) ≈ GPTQ (0.9886) > GPTQ+SmoothQuant (0.9808) > RTN (0.9775) > SmoothQuant (0.9739). AWQ and GPTQ tie on average but fail differently — AWQ owns math_500 (1.0262) and is weak on gpqa (0.9485); GPTQ has the higher floor (0.9706).
- **SmoothQuant is the weakest family, and GPTQ repairs it.** Its gsm8k collapse (0.9227, the worst gsm8k cell in the sweep) goes to 0.9913 with GPTQ on top — it pushes difficulty from activations into weights, and GPTQ cleans those up.
- **RTN is the bar.** 0.9775 with no weight-side algorithm at all; `NVFP4-ultrachat` holds the best mmlu_pro result anywhere (0.9997). A calibrated recipe that does not clear 0.9775 is not paying for itself.
- **Read gpqa and aime25 loosely.** Their seed-noise floors are ±0.0490 and ±0.0807, so most gaps in those two columns are unresolvable at these seed counts. Recoveries >1.0 (6 on math_500) are "no measurable loss," not gains; only AWQ on math_500 clears its floor.

 Issue body actions 

 Qwen3-30B-A3B NVFP4 Evaluation Results (MoE, reasoning)

 Setup

 Baseline — Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B (BF16)

 Variants — 12 NVFP4 W4A4 models, all nvfp4-pack-quantized , group_size 16, tensor_group strategy:

 All 48 mlp.gate routers and lm_head left unquantized.

 GPTQ variants additionally use actorder: static on weights.

 -ultrachat variants are calibrated on UltraChat; all others on the default perfect-blend mix.

 Plain NVFP4 is round-to-nearest on the weights — its calibration pass only fits the activation global scales.

 Seeds — 1234, 2345, 3456. aime25 additionally uses 4567, 5678, 6789, 7890, 8901.

 Sampling — temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95, top_k 20

 Serving — vLLM v0.28.0, TP=1 (one GPU per model), max-model-len 40960, no YaRN, --reasoning-parser qwen3 

 Harness — eval-agent --category reasoning , num_concurrent 32

 Recovery — seed-mean(quantized) / seed-mean(BF16), per task, on the metric listed for that task. Every score in this document is a mean over seeds.

 NVFP4-GPTQ-AWQ-ultrachat — Default pick. Best mean of any variant, and best mean rank across the five tasks (4.2 of 12). Holds the best aime25 in the sweep (0.9882) and 2nd-best mmlu_pro (0.9986); its softest column is math_500 (0.9883, rank 10 of 12), which is the one thing 2 covers better.

 NVFP4-AWQ — Math-heavy workloads. Best math_500 anywhere (1.0262) and best ifeval of the three (0.9969), but the weakest mmlu_pro of the three by a wide margin (0.9601 vs 0.9986) — do not ship it where knowledge/multiple-choice matters.

 NVFP4-GPTQ-ultrachat — Highest floor. Its worst task (0.9588) is the best worst-task of all 12 variants, and it holds the best gpqa in the sweep (0.9742). Pick it when the requirement is "nothing regresses badly" rather than a peak on any one task.

 Caveats: 2 and 3 are separated by 0.0011 mean recovery, which is inside the noise on every task that drives it — treat them as tied on aggregate and choose on the profile above. Dropping gsm8k is what promotes NVFP4-AWQ into the top three; on gsm8k it is the third-weakest variant (0.9754), so keep it out of the running if gsm8k-style CoT arithmetic ever matters. NVFP4-GPTQ-AWQ-ultrachat leads with or without gsm8k.

 Takeaways

 NVFP4-GPTQ-AWQ-ultrachat is the pick — best mean recovery (0.9885), wins gsm8k (1.0067) and aime25 (0.9882).

 The BF16 gap is concentrated in gpqa_diamond and aime25. Mean recovery across the 12 variants: gpqa 0.9439 and aime25 0.9451 — 5.6 and 5.5 pts short of BF16 — vs mmlu_pro 0.9787, gsm8k 0.9813, ifeval 0.9934 and math_500 1.0010 (no loss). Those two are the only tasks where NVFP4 gives up real ground, and they are the worst task for 11 of 12 variants.

 GPTQ is what closes that gap. On gpqa the top four variants are all GPTQ recipes (0.9742 / 0.9639 / 0.9588 / 0.9536, vs 0.9510 for the best non-GPTQ), and a GPTQ recipe tops aime25 too (0.9882, though 2 there is plain AWQ at 0.9765). Mean rank of the 6 GPTQ vs 6 non-GPTQ variants is 4.8 vs 8.2 on gpqa and 5.7 vs 7.3 on aime25. The individual deltas sit inside those tasks' noise floors (±0.0490, ±0.0807), but the ranking is consistent across both — the one exception is GPTQ+SmoothQuant , last on both (0.9046, 0.9059).

 GPTQ's other effects: its one resolvable win elsewhere is gsm8k (+0.0280 averaged over the 6 recipe pairs, positive in 6 of 6, 2.5x the noise floor); it costs mmlu_pro (−0.0102, ~3x that task's floor) and math_500 (−0.0131).

 UltraChat vs perfect-blend is a wash on average (+0.0038) but directional per task: clearly better on mmlu_pro (+0.0231, positive for all 6 recipes), clearly worse on math_500 (−0.0148). Use UltraChat unless math is the target.

 Calibration matters most for the smoothing recipes. GPTQ+AWQ swings 0.9720 → 0.9885 on calibration alone — largest spread in the sweep. GPTQ is indifferent (0.0004). RTN is the recipe UltraChat hurts most (−0.0130), which fits: it has no per-channel scales to fit.

 Recipe order: GPTQ+AWQ (0.9902) > AWQ (0.9888) ≈ GPTQ (0.9886) > GPTQ+SmoothQuant (0.9808) > RTN (0.9775) > SmoothQuant (0.9739). AWQ and GPTQ tie on average but fail differently — AWQ owns math_500 (1.0262) and is weak on gpqa (0.9485); GPTQ has the higher floor (0.9706).

 SmoothQuant is the weakest family, and GPTQ repairs it. Its gsm8k collapse (0.9227, the worst gsm8k cell in the sweep) goes to 0.9913 with GPTQ on top — it pushes difficulty from activations into weights, and GPTQ cleans those up.

 RTN is the bar. 0.9775 with no weight-side algorithm at all; NVFP4-ultrachat holds the best mmlu_pro result anywhere (0.9997). A calibrated recipe that does not clear 0.9775 is not paying for itself.

 Read gpqa and aime25 loosely. Their seed-noise floors are ±0.0490 and ±0.0807, so most gaps in those two columns are unresolvable at these seed counts. Recoveries >1.0 (6 on math_500) are "no measurable loss," not gains; only AWQ on math_500 clears its floor.
