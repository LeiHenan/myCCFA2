--- COMMENT 1 ---
Will the new nvidia blackwell architecture  be supported? For example, the rtx 50xx series.

--- COMMENT 2 ---
@ZhuYe0729  Yes. The kernels compiled for cuda and is forward compatible to any future gpu by the `nvcc` compiler. However, there are  currently no gptq kernel that is fully optimized for the consumer `blackwell` 50XX gpus.

--- COMMENT 3 ---
@Qubitium when you say “there are currently no gptq kernel that is fully optimized for the consumer blackwell 50XX gpus” do you mean it will still run on a 5090 but it is just not optimized?

--- COMMENT 4 ---
@jordan-wriker When I said optimized I meant running on consumer Blackwell at close to max utilization. AI kernels is not like gaming where code does not have to be hand tuned for each GPU generation. It will run well, just not at max potential is the jest of it. We will monitor open source space for Blackwell optimized kernels.
