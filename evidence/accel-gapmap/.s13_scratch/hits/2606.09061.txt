 Abstract

 As large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed with highly heterogeneous workloads, chunked-prefill execution has emerged as a mainstream serving architecture. Balancing scheduling fairness and latency stability in such environments is critical; otherwise, severe head-of-line blocking and request starvation will degrade user experience. However, existing systems rely on rigid First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) policies and static token budgets, leading to fairness degradation and unpredictable latency jitter. To address these issues, we propose a fairness-aware and latency-controllable scheduling framework for chunked-prefill LLM engines.
