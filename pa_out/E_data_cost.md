**Distillation / self-distillation**
- "identifying a compact draft model that is well-aligned with the target model is challenging… DistillSpec makes two key design choices, which we demonstrate via systematic study to be crucial to improving the draft and target alignment: utilizing on-policy data generation from the draft model, and tailoring the divergence function to the task and decoding strategy." — Zhou et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08461 , Abstract.
- "The main idea is to continuously update the (multiple) draft model(s) on observed user query data. Adapting to query distribution mitigates the shifts between the training distribution of the draft model and the query distribution, enabling the draft model to more accurately predict the target model's outputs." — Liu et al. (OSD), https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07177 , Abstract.
- "Our results show a substantial increase in the token acceptance rate by 0.1 to 0.65, bringing 1.42x to 2.17x latency reduction." — Liu et al. (OSD), same URL, Abstract.
- "DraftExpert trains one lightweight accelerator-resident draft expert per layer by self-distilling residual, logit/token, and router-agreement signals from the frozen target MoE." — DraftExpert, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24434 , Abstract.
- "we propose AdaFlash framework, comprising two components: (i) an on-policy distillation (OPD) algorithm with reverse-KL divergence tailored for diffusion drafters, bringing stable convergence and effectively reducing domain-level variance" — AdaFlash, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19223 , Abstract.
- "This decoupling ensures that training never blocks token generation." — AdaFlash, same URL, §3.4 (infrastructure).
