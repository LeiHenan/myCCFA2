In DSATokenToKVPool, self.index_k_with_scale_buffer is page-indexed with shape (num_pages, cols), where num_pages is scaled down by self.page_size (which is 64 on CUDA). However, tgt_loc_flat and src_loc_flat contain token-level indices (ranging up to size + page_size). Indexing index_k directly with these token indices will cause a guaranteed IndexError: index out of range on CUDA. To fix this, the token indices must be divided by self.page_size to obtain the correct page indices before indexing index_k.

 
 
 
 Suggested change

 continue

 

 
 

 index_k[tgt_loc_flat] = index_k[src_loc_flat]

 src_page_flat = src_loc_flat // self.page_size
