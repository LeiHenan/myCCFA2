🐛 Describe the bug

vLLM 0.8.3/0.8.4:

In v0 mode, ngram speculative decoding works as expected.
In v1 mode, ngram speculative decoding does not work.

vLLM 0.7.3:

Both v0/v1 mode work as expected.

Let me know if I missed anything.
